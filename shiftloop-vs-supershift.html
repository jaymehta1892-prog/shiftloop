Shiftloop vs Supershift

Supershift and Shiftloop are both private, no-account shift calendars for iPhone with rotations, pay tracking, widgets and Apple Watch. The differences are in how your roster gets in and how far the pay side goes.

Updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Choose Supershift if you want a proven calendar with years of polish, several shifts on one day, automatic wake-up alarms per shift type, and PDF or calendar export.
  • Choose Shiftloop if your roster arrives as a photo, screenshot or PDF and you want it read for you, or you want pay worked out like a payslip: pay periods, your next payday, holiday pay and pay rises that start on a set date.

Where Supershift is stronger

Supershift has been around for years and it shows: a well-tested calendar, several shift types on one day, wake-up alarms set automatically for each shift type, PDF export of a month, and widgets in several sizes. If you mostly want a calendar and alarms, it's a strong choice.

Where Shiftloop is stronger

Your roster, read for you. Photograph the roster on the wall, or pick a screenshot or a PDF. Shiftloop reads the dates and shift codes on your iPhone, asks which row is yours, and adds the shifts. If the roster lists several workplaces, it can turn them into separate jobs.

Pay like a payslip. Shiftloop works in your pay periods (weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly), shows what's coming in your next paycheck, adds holiday pay on public holidays, and lets a pay rise start on the right date without changing what past shifts paid.

Teams. Coworkers can join a team with a code to see who's on and swap shifts, still without accounts.

Common questions

Is Shiftloop like Supershift?

Yes in spirit: both are private shift calendars with no account, rotations, pay tracking, widgets and Apple Watch. Shiftloop adds reading your roster from a photo and payslip-style pay (pay periods, payday, holiday pay); Supershift has alarms per shift type and PDF export.

Can Supershift import a roster from a photo?

Its App Store listing (November 2026) doesn't mention it. Shiftloop reads rosters from photos, screenshots and PDFs on your iPhone.

Can I move my shifts from Supershift to Shiftloop?

There's no direct import. The quickest way is to set up your rotation in Shiftloop, or scan your current roster.
