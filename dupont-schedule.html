DuPont schedule (12‑hour rotating shifts)

The DuPont schedule is a four-week rotation of 12-hour shifts that ends with seven days off in a row. Across the 28 days you work 14 shifts: seven days and seven nights.

How the DuPont schedule works

  • Week 1: four night shifts, then three days off.
  • Week 2: three day shifts, one day off, then three night shifts.
  • Week 3: three days off, then four day shifts.
  • Week 4: seven days off.

That is the order with the cycle starting on a Monday. Four crews run the same cycle a week apart, so every shift is covered.

It takes its name from the DuPont company, which used it in plants that run around the clock.

Hours and days off

A 28-day cycle holds 14 shifts and 14 days off. With 12-hour shifts that is 168 hours a cycle, an average of 42 hours a week.

Over a year it comes to about 182 shifts and about 2,184 hours. The longest run is 4 shifts in a row, and the longest break is 7 days.

What people like

  • Seven days off in a row every four weeks
  • An average of 42 hours a week
  • Days and nights are shared out evenly

What to watch

  • Week 2 holds 72 hours: three days, one day off, then three nights
  • A quick change from days to nights in week 2, with a single day off between them
  • The long break is paid for with heavy working weeks

Common questions

How many hours a week is the DuPont schedule?

42 hours a week on average with 12-hour shifts: 14 shifts in every 28 days.

How many days off do you get on the DuPont schedule?

14 days off in every 28-day cycle, which is about 183 days off a year.

How many days off in a row do you get on the DuPont schedule?

Seven, at the end of every 28-day cycle. There are also two three-day breaks and a single day off earlier in the cycle.

Can I put the DuPont schedule on my phone?

Yes. Shiftloop for iPhone has it built in as “DuPont”. Choose it under Repeat pattern, say where you are in the cycle, and it fills your calendar as far ahead as you need. The app is free to download.
