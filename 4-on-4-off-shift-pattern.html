How the 4 on 4 off shift pattern works

The cycle is eight days long and a week is seven, so your working days move forward by one day each time round. Over eight weeks you work every day of the week the same number of times, weekends included.

Workplaces run it in a few ways. Some keep you on days or on nights. Some alternate: four days, four off, four nights, four off, which makes a 16-day cycle. Another version is two days followed by two nights. The buttons above the calendar switch between them.

Hours and days off

A 8-day cycle holds 4 shifts and 4 days off. With 12-hour shifts that is 48 hours a cycle, an average of 42 hours a week.

Over a year it comes to about 182 shifts and about 2,184 hours. The longest run is 4 shifts in a row, and the longest break is 4 days.

What people like

  • Four days off after every run of shifts
  • The same rhythm all year, so it's easy to plan ahead
  • You work half the days in the year

What to watch

  • Days off move through the week, so they only sometimes fall on a weekend
  • Four 12-hour shifts in a row is a long run
  • Public holidays land on working days as often as on days off

Common questions

How many hours a week is the 4 on 4 off shift pattern?

42 hours a week on average with 12-hour shifts: 4 shifts in every 8 days.

How many days off do you get on the 4 on 4 off shift pattern?

4 days off in every 8-day cycle, which is about 183 days off a year.

Is 4 on 4 off the same as the Continental shift pattern?

They are close. Both are eight-day cycles with four days off. In Shiftloop, 4 on 4 off is four of the same shift, and Continental is two days followed by two nights.

Can I put the 4 on 4 off shift pattern on my phone?

Yes. Shiftloop for iPhone has it built in as “4 on / 4 off”. Choose it under Repeat pattern, say where you are in the cycle, and it fills your calendar as far ahead as you need. The app is free to download.
