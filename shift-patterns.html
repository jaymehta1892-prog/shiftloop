Rotating shift schedules

Shift patterns and rotating schedules

A shift pattern is the cycle of working days and days off that repeats through the year. These are the ones people ask about most. Each page shows the cycle, the hours it adds up to, and a calendar you can start on your own date.

How to read a shift pattern

Patterns are usually written as runs of days on and days off. "4 on 4 off" is four shifts, then four days off. "2-2-3" is two on, two off, three on, then the mirror image the week after.

Three numbers tell you most of what you need: how long the cycle is, how many shifts it holds, and how long each shift lasts. From those you get the average hours a week, which is what your contract and your overtime are usually measured against.

If your workplace runs something else, Shiftloop lets you build your own cycle, or tap shifts onto the days one by one.
