Overtime on 12-hour shifts, explained

Three 12-hour shifts make a 36-hour week, so most weeks on a 12-hour rotation have no overtime at all. Then a four-shift week comes along, and whether you're owed overtime depends on where you work and how your employer counts the week.

Updated November 2026

The two ways overtime is counted

Weekly overtime counts the hours in a week and pays extra on the hours past a limit, such as 40 or 44. Daily overtime pays extra on the hours past a limit in one day, such as 8 or 12. Some places use both, and you get whichever gives more overtime, not both on the same hour.

On 12-hour shifts the difference is big. With weekly overtime, a 36-hour week has none. With daily overtime after 8 hours, every 12-hour shift has 4 overtime hours.

These are the minimums the law sets, as of November 2026. Union agreements and contracts often pay more, for example overtime for any time past your scheduled shift. Some workplaces have approved arrangements, such as 12-hour shift agreements, that change the daily limit. Always check your own contract or collective agreement.

Worked examples

For a nurse on $40 an hour working 12-hour shifts:

  • US, 3 shifts in a week (36 hours): no overtime. Pay: 36 × $40 = $1,440.
  • US, 4 shifts in a week (48 hours): 8 hours past 40. Pay: 40 × $40 + 8 × $60 = $2,080.
  • Ontario, 4 shifts in a week (48 hours): 4 hours past 44. Pay: 44 × $40 + 4 × $60 = $2,000.
  • British Columbia, 1 shift with no daily-overtime agreement: 8 regular hours and 4 overtime hours. Pay: 8 × $40 + 4 × $60 = $560.

Where overtime goes missing

  • The workweek doesn't start on Monday. Employers set their own workweek. Four shifts across Saturday to Wednesday might fall in one week or be split over two.
  • A shift runs past midnight. A night shift's hours can be counted on the day it started or split across two days, which changes daily totals.
  • Staying late. Twenty minutes of charting after every shift adds up, and on a long week it's overtime.
  • Premiums on top. Night and weekend differentials are usually part of the regular rate that overtime is worked out from, so overtime hours should include them.

Common questions

Do you get overtime for a 12-hour shift?

Not always. Where overtime is weekly (US federal rules, Ontario, Quebec), a single 12-hour shift has none; overtime starts once the week passes 40 or 44 hours. Where overtime is daily (California, BC, Alberta, federally regulated work in Canada), the hours past 8 in a day are overtime, unless an approved agreement changes the daily limit.

How many hours is overtime in Ontario?

Under Ontario's Employment Standards Act, overtime is paid at 1.5 times the regular rate for hours past 44 in a work week. Many collective agreements, including for nurses, pay overtime sooner.

Do nurses get overtime after 8 hours?

It depends on the place and the contract. US federal law only requires weekly overtime, but hospitals can use the 8/80 system, and California requires overtime after 8 hours in a day. In Canada and the UK, union and NHS agreements set it.
