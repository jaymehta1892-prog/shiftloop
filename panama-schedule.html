How the Panama schedule works

Each fortnight runs 2 on, 2 off, 3 on, then 2 off, 2 on, 3 off. With the cycle starting on a Monday, you work Monday and Tuesday, then Friday to Sunday, and the week after only Wednesday and Thursday.

After 14 days you switch from day shifts to night shifts and run the same fortnight again. Every other weekend is a three-day weekend, whichever half you are on.

Workplaces differ on how often crews swap between days and nights. If yours swaps at a different point, you can build it in Shiftloop as your own cycle. The Pitman schedule has the same days on and off without the swap.

Hours and days off

A 28-day cycle holds 14 shifts and 14 days off. With 12-hour shifts that is 168 hours a cycle, an average of 42 hours a week.

Over a year it comes to about 182 shifts and about 2,184 hours. The longest run is 3 shifts in a row, and the longest break is 3 days.

What people like

  • Every other weekend is a three-day weekend
  • Never more than three shifts in a row
  • Two full weeks on the same shift before you swap

What to watch

  • 12-hour shifts are long
  • Switching between days and nights every two weeks takes adjusting
  • Hours are uneven from week to week

Common questions

How many hours a week is the Panama schedule?

42 hours a week on average with 12-hour shifts: 14 shifts in every 28 days.

How many days off do you get on the Panama schedule?

14 days off in every 28-day cycle, which is about 183 days off a year.

Is the Panama schedule the same as 2-2-3?

Yes. 2-2-3 describes the run of days: two on, two off, three on. Panama is the best-known name for it.

What is the difference between the Panama and Pitman schedules?

The days on and off are the same. The names are used loosely, but in Shiftloop the Panama alternates two weeks of day shifts with two weeks of night shifts, while the Pitman keeps one kind of shift.

Can I put the Panama schedule on my phone?

Yes. Shiftloop for iPhone has it built in as “Panama”. Choose it under Repeat pattern, say where you are in the cycle, and it fills your calendar as far ahead as you need. The app is free to download.
