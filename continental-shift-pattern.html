How the Continental shift pattern works

You work two day shifts, go straight on to two night shifts, then have four days off. Like any eight-day cycle, it moves forward by one day of the week each time round.

Some workplaces use the name for a different rotation: 8-hour morning, afternoon and night shifts in runs of two, two and three, over four weeks. If that is yours, this calendar won't match it. In Shiftloop you can still add those shifts by tapping them onto the days.

Hours and days off

A 8-day cycle holds 4 shifts and 4 days off. With 12-hour shifts that is 48 hours a cycle, an average of 42 hours a week.

Over a year it comes to about 182 shifts and about 2,184 hours. The longest run is 4 shifts in a row, and the longest break is 4 days.

What people like

  • Four days off after every four shifts
  • Days and nights are shared out evenly
  • The same rhythm all year

What to watch

  • You go from day shifts to night shifts inside the same run
  • Days off move through the week, so they only sometimes fall on a weekend
  • 12-hour shifts are long

Common questions

How many hours a week is the Continental shift pattern?

42 hours a week on average with 12-hour shifts: 4 shifts in every 8 days.

How many days off do you get on the Continental shift pattern?

4 days off in every 8-day cycle, which is about 183 days off a year.

How is Continental different from 4 on 4 off?

Both are four shifts followed by four days off. In Shiftloop, Continental splits the four shifts into two days and two nights, and 4 on 4 off is four of the same shift.

Can I put the Continental shift pattern on my phone?

Yes. Shiftloop for iPhone has it built in as “Continental”. Choose it under Repeat pattern, say where you are in the cycle, and it fills your calendar as far ahead as you need. The app is free to download.
