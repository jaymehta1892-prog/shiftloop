Roster scanning

Turn a photo of your roster into your calendar

Most rosters still arrive as a printout on the staff-room wall, a screenshot in the group chat or a PDF in your email. Shiftloop reads it for you: take a photo, choose your name, and your shifts are on your calendar.

Updated November 2026

How it works

  • Take a photo or pick a file. A photo of the printed roster, a screenshot, or a PDF. Crooked or sideways photos are straightened first.
  • Choose your row. On a team roster with everyone's names, Shiftloop asks which row is yours. On your own schedule, it skips this.
  • Check and add. You see every day it found, with the shift each code became. Unknown codes become new shift types you can rename. Then add them all at once, and undo with one tap if something's off.

What it can read

  • Team rosters in a grid: names down the side, dates across the top, shift codes in the cells (D, N, E, L, 7–3, 0700–1930 and so on).
  • Lists of dates with times, like the schedule screens of most staffing and HR apps.
  • Rosters that cover several workplaces, which it can turn into separate jobs with their own pay.
  • Days off and leave codes such as OFF, RDO, AL, VAC and PTO.

Printed and typed rosters work best. Handwriting is hit and miss.

Private by design

The roster is read on your iPhone with Apple's own text recognition. It isn't uploaded anywhere and the picture isn't kept, so your coworkers' names stay off any server. Only the shifts you choose to add are saved, in your calendar and your own iCloud.

Then it works out your pay

Once the shifts are in, Shiftloop shows what each one pays with your hourly rate, overtime and premiums, and what's coming in your next paycheck.

Common questions

Is there an app that turns a photo of my work schedule into a calendar?

Yes. Shiftloop for iPhone reads a photo, screenshot or PDF of your roster, finds your shifts, and adds them to its calendar. It can also put them in Apple's Calendar app.

Does roster scanning upload my roster?

No. It's read on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition, and the image isn't kept.

Is roster scanning free?

Your first three scans are free. Unlimited scans are part of Shiftloop Pro, which has a one-month free trial.

What if the scan gets a shift wrong?

You check every day before adding, can leave days out, and can undo the whole scan with one tap.
