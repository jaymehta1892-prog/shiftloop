Shiftloop vs NurseGrid

Both are free iPhone shift calendars used by nurses. NurseGrid is a nurse network with a calendar; Shiftloop is a calendar that works out what your shifts pay. Which one fits depends on what you want from the app.

Updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Choose NurseGrid if most of your unit already uses it, and you want to see who you're working with, message colleagues, pick up open shifts or look for jobs.
  • Choose Shiftloop if you want your pay checked: what each shift earns with overtime, night and weekend premiums and holiday pay, and what's in your next paycheck. Also if your roster comes as a photo, a screenshot or a PDF and you'd rather not type it in.
  • Plenty of nurses use both: NurseGrid for the unit, Shiftloop for the money.

Where NurseGrid is stronger

NurseGrid's strength is the network. If your unit is on it, you can see who's on your shift, compare calendars with colleagues, message them, and pick up open shifts and swaps across it. It also lists nursing jobs. Shiftloop has none of that beyond swaps inside a team you set up yourself.

Where Shiftloop is stronger

Pay. Put in your hourly rate and your overtime rule (daily, weekly or both), and every shift shows what it earns, including night, weekend and holiday premiums. The Earnings tab shows the pay period, the month and the year, and your next payday. Nurses use it to check their paycheck for missed overtime.

Getting your roster in. Take a photo of the printed roster, or pick a screenshot or PDF. Shiftloop reads the dates and shift codes on your iPhone, finds your row, and adds the shifts. Your first three scans are free.

Privacy. There's no account. Your shifts and pay stay on your phone and in your own iCloud, and the app has no ads or analytics.

Switching or using both

Nothing needs exporting. Install Shiftloop, set up your shift types in the one-minute setup, and either repeat your rotation or scan your next roster. You can keep NurseGrid for your unit at the same time.

Common questions

Is Shiftloop a good NurseGrid alternative?

If you mainly want a calendar that also works out your pay and overtime, yes. If you rely on NurseGrid's nurse network (seeing coworkers, messaging, open shifts, jobs), Shiftloop doesn't replace that, and many nurses keep both.

Does NurseGrid calculate overtime pay?

NurseGrid's App Store listing (November 2026) doesn't mention pay or overtime calculations. Shiftloop does: daily and weekly overtime, night and weekend premiums, holiday pay and your next payday.

Is Shiftloop free?

Yes. The calendar, rotations, reminders, swaps and pay tracking for one job are free. Shiftloop Pro adds several jobs, unlimited roster scans, widgets, Apple Watch, live sharing and pay history.

Is Shiftloop on Android?

Not yet. It's for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
