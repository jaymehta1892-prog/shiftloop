How do I restore Shiftloop Pro on a new phone?

Open Settings → Get Shiftloop Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Shiftloop Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

My shifts aren't syncing between devices

Check you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices, and that iCloud is on for Shiftloop in the Settings app → your name → iCloud.

How do I delete my data?

In Shiftloop, open Settings → Erase all data. The privacy policy explains how to remove the copy in iCloud and any team data.
