Getting started

Three ways to get your shift schedule in

Pick whichever suits your roster. Most people are set up in about a minute.

Repeat your rotation

Choose 4-on-4-off, Continental, Pitman, Panama, DuPont, 5-on-2-off or 24-on-48-off, or build your own cycle. Set it once and it runs as far ahead as you need.

Tap the days

No fixed pattern? Pick a shift, then tap each day it falls on. Tap again to take it off.

Photograph the roster

Take a photo, or choose a screenshot or PDF. Shiftloop reads the dates and codes on your iPhone, nothing is uploaded, and you check the days before anything is added.

Your first 3 scans are free.

Is Shiftloop free?

Yes. The calendar, repeating patterns, reminders, shift swaps and pay tracking for one job are free with no time limit. Shiftloop Pro adds multiple jobs, unlimited roster scans, widgets and Apple Watch, live sharing and pay history.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Shiftloop is for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, on iOS 18 or later.

How do I restore Shiftloop Pro on a new phone?

Open Settings → Get Shiftloop Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Shiftloop Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

My shifts aren't syncing between devices

Check you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices, and that iCloud is on for Shiftloop in the Settings app → your name → iCloud.

How do I delete my data?

In Shiftloop, open Settings → Erase all data. The privacy policy explains how to remove the copy in iCloud and any team data.
