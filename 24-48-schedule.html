24 on 48 off schedule (24/48 firefighter shifts)

On a 24/48 schedule you work one 24-hour shift, then have 48 hours off. It is the classic firefighter rotation, usually run by three crews called A, B and C shift.

How the 24/48 schedule works

The cycle is three days long: one day on, two days off. Three crews take turns, so there is always one on duty. The buttons above the calendar show each crew's days.

Many departments add an extra day off every few weeks, often called a Kelly day, to bring the average hours down. To see that on the calendar, tap the day to take the shift off.

Hours and days off

A 3-day cycle holds 1 shift and 2 days off. With 24-hour shifts that is 24 hours a cycle, an average of 56 hours a week.

Over a year it comes to about 122 shifts and about 2,912 hours. The longest run is 1 shift in a row, and the longest break is 2 days.

What people like

  • Two full days off after every shift
  • The same three-day rhythm all year
  • Only about 122 trips to work a year

What to watch

  • 24 hours on duty at a time
  • 56 hours a week on average, more than most rotations
  • You work a Saturday or a Sunday two weeks out of three

Common questions

How many hours a week is the 24/48 schedule?

56 hours a week on average with 24-hour shifts: 1 shift in every 3 days.

How many days off do you get on the 24/48 schedule?

2 days off in every 3-day cycle, which is about 243 days off a year.

How many hours a year is a 24/48 schedule?

About 2,912: an average of 56 hours a week, or about 122 24-hour shifts a year, before any Kelly days.

Can I put the 24/48 schedule on my phone?

Yes. Shiftloop for iPhone has it built in as “24 on / 48 off”. Choose it under Repeat pattern, say where you are in the cycle, and it fills your calendar as far ahead as you need. The app is free to download.
